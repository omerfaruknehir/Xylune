Open-source BYOK AI chat for Android

Your AI.
Your providers.
One native app.

Turp (pronounced “Zy-loon”) is a native, local-first Android chat client. Bring your own API keys, supported provider accounts, or local model servers and keep one workspace instead of being locked into a single AI service.

hub

Connect what you already use

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, xAI, ChatGPT, OpenAI-compatible endpoints, and compatible local model servers.

shield

Private by design

Chats and credentials stay on your device. Requests go directly to the provider or server you choose—there is no Turp relay backend.

phone_android

Actually native Android

Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Material 3, with streaming chat, search, files, Markdown, LaTeX, Python, and optional Linux tools.

Bring your own key

BYOK without the friction

BYOK means bring your own key. Turp does not sell model access: connect a provider you already use, choose your models, and keep the Android interface and local workspace separate from the provider.
